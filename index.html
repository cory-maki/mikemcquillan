About Mike

Marine, teacher, builder, author.

Mike McQuillan enlisted in the U.S. Marine Corps at nineteen and spent four years as a paralegal in Okinawa, Japan. The discipline stayed. The fascination with Asia stayed. What changed was the work — Mac came to Taiwan to teach, and a decade later, he is still in the classroom.

In Beitun, Taichung, McQuillan helped build two schools from the ground up: Exemplar English, where he shaped the curriculum and assessment frameworks, and Adventureland, where he designed the identity, materials, and culture. Along the way he started writing — first lessons, then textbooks, then a 12-volume storybook series called Adventures in Mr. Mac's Class, in which a magical watch turns every chapter into a field trip.

His path from Marine paralegal to Taiwan classroom doesn't read in a straight line, and that's the point. Mac believes every student can learn, and every classroom can be an adventure — when the teacher is willing to build the thing.
